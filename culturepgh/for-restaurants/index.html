For Restaurants

Share your story with Pittsburgh

CulturePGH is a volunteer project that helps locals find immigrant-owned restaurants and get to know the people behind them. If you own one, you choose how involved you want to be.

Get in touch
Three Ways to Take Part

Pick the level that fits

Start wherever you're comfortable. You can always do more later.

  1. Listed in the guide

    Your restaurant appears in the CulturePGH guide with your cuisine, neighborhood, address, and links.

    • Shown on the guide's map and in its filters

  2. Full feature

    We visit, take professional photos of your food, and write up your story with you: how you came to Pittsburgh and what inspires your cooking.

    • Your own profile page with an owner bio and photo gallery
    • Marked as a featured partner in the guide
    • Shared with our email list

  3. Host a CulturePGH dinner

    A ticketed evening where guests hear the owner's story over a multi-course meal, with proceeds supporting the restaurant.

    • You decide the menu
    • Photos from the night that you're welcome to use
