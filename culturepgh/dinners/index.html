The Evening

How a dinner works

Each dinner is a little different, because each restaurant decides its own menu. Here's the general shape of the night.

First Course

Sit down and eat

We mix up the seating so you end up talking with people you haven't met. Food starts coming out soon after everyone arrives.

Second Course

Hear the story

Between courses, the owner or someone in their family talks about what we're eating, where it's from, and how they ended up cooking it in Pittsburgh.

Third Course

Ask questions

After the talk there's time for questions while dessert comes out.
